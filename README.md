# AtendeAI — AI Lead Qualification

AI-powered lead qualification automation built with n8n and Generative AI.

## 📌 Overview

AtendeAI is an automation workflow designed to assist businesses in the first stage of customer service and lead qualification.

The AI Agent conducts a conversation with the customer, collects relevant information, organizes the data and prepares a structured lead for the sales team.

## ⚙️ Workflow

Customer message  
↓  
n8n  
↓  
AI Agent  
↓  
Information collection  
↓  
Lead qualification  
↓  
Structured lead data  
↓  
Sales team

## 🤖 Features

- Automated customer conversations
- Lead qualification
- Collection of customer requirements
- Structured data extraction
- AI-generated responses
